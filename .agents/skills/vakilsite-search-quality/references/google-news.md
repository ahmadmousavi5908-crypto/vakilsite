# Google News publisher references

- Google News technical guidelines: https://support.google.com/news/publisher-center/answer/9606708
- Google News content policies: https://support.google.com/news/publisher-center/answer/6204050
- News sitemap: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/news-sitemap
- Article structured data: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article

A legal website is not automatically a news publisher. Evaluate editorial staffing, original reporting, transparency, permanent URLs, dates/bylines, contact details, and correction policy before any Google News-specific implementation. News sitemaps include only URLs for articles created within the last two days.
