# Vakilsite Knowledge Registry

## Purpose

This directory is the central inventory for the website's existing knowledge
sources and the imported SEO/MAVIS research documents.

## Existing website knowledge

The website already has a structured legal library, legal glossary, articles,
practice areas, taxonomies, record relationships and source-review data.
These are the existing foundations. Do not create parallel copies without
a demonstrated need.

## Research documents

- `docs/seo/`: internal editorial and search-discovery research.
- `docs/mavis/`: internal MAVIS product, architecture, legal intelligence,
  security, operations and research documentation.

These documents are references, not public website pages.

## Access and legal safeguards

- Public content sources may be reused only according to their actual
  publication status.
- Legal materials must be checked against authoritative and current sources.
- Internal SEO and MAVIS documents must not be exposed to visitors.
- Legal answers must preserve source identity and traceability.
- No legal authority, citation or court decision may be invented.

## Machine-readable inventory

See [`manifest.json`](./manifest.json) for document paths, titles, headings,
access levels, existing data sources and future retrieval requirements.

## Implementation status

This registry does not implement an AI retrieval engine. It does not connect
MAVIS to an AI provider, generate embeddings, create a vector database or
publish content. It prepares a controlled inventory for that later work.

## Preservation

This registry does not replace or modify existing website content, page
components, styles, routes or the current legal library.
