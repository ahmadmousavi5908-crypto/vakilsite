# Changelog

## 1.3.1 - 2026-11-02
- Expanded the canonical glossary from 45 to 132 terms.
- Preserved 57 unique legacy glossary entries, merged 7 overlapping entries and added 30 cross-topic terms.
- Expanded taxonomy coverage to 19 categories and 353 curated Persian search phrases.
- Synchronized the manifest and documentation with the integrated dataset counts.
- Kept legal review statuses conservative; no record was promoted to primary-source verified by this data migration.

## 1.2.1 - 2026-11-01
- Fixed release packaging so MANIFEST.json is included in every downloadable archive.
- Strengthened validation to require the manifest, verify its file inventory and compare declared record counts with actual data.
- Added package self-checks for exact archive contents, manifest consistency and ZIP integrity.
- No legal dataset records or website files were changed.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-01
- Added nine Registration Law article summaries covering compulsory registration, evidentiary effects, unregistered property, urban partition and cadastral mapping.
- Added references from core property terms to the new article records.
- Recorded the interpretive distinction in Advisory Opinion 7/1402/792 concerning Article 88.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-01
- Added source-review findings for the phased launch of the Article 10 claim-registration system.
- Clarified that Article 1 consequences must be assessed by property document date and the officially announced launch scope.
- Added summaries for Article 10 of the compulsory real-estate registration law and Articles 4 and 21 bis of the Cheque Issuance Law.
- Recorded the 1400/01/29 cheque-law amendment affecting the Sayad registration and transfer rules.
- Linked cheque and unregistered-property terms to the additional legal provisions.
- Added deterministic ZIP packaging and SHA-256 generation for versioned releases.
- Added a GitHub Actions workflow to validate and publish release assets.
- Kept legal statuses at secondary-source level; no blanket primary-source consolidation claim is made.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-01
- Created an independent, data-only Iranian legal research library.
- Prioritized real-estate registration and property terminology.
- Included cheque, Sayad cheque and promissory-note terminology in v1.
- Added civil law, civil procedure, criminal law, article summaries, taxonomy and relations.
- Added a Python standard-library validator suitable for Termux.
- Marked records for legal review where primary-source consolidation is incomplete.
